At what age can a rhinoplasty be done?

ASOCLICPER TeamColombian Association of Plastic, Aesthetic and Reconstructive Surgery Clinics
Leer este artículo en español

Before knowing at what age you can have a rhinoplasty, we should first clarify what Rhinoplasty is. Rhinoplasty is a cosmetic surgery performed to improve the appearance and/or function of the nose. It is one of the most popular cosmetic surgeries and is performed on people of all ages, however, it is important to understand that age is an important factor to consider before undergoing rhinoplasty.

In general terms, answering the question regarding the age rhinoplasty can be done, rhinoplasty can be performed on people who have reached facial maturity, which generally occurs around the age of 18. At this age, the structure of the nose has already reached its final size and shape and any alterations made to it are considered permanent.

There are some exceptions to this rule of at what age rhinoplasty can be performed. In cases where there is a congenital deformity or a significant respiratory problem, surgery may be recommended at a younger age. In these cases, surgery is performed to correct an abnormality and improve the function of the nose.

In addition, some adolescents may experience emotional disorders related to their physical appearance. While this is not a medical reason to perform rhinoplasty, plastic surgeons may consider surgery in these cases, as long as the teenager has an understanding of the results to be expected, in addition to being both emotionally and physically prepared for the procedure, and having prior consent from their parents or legal guardian.

Ultimately, determining the proper age to undergo a rhinoplasty should be a well thought out decision that ought to be thoroughly discussed with an experienced plastic surgeon. The surgeon can evaluate the person’s facial structure and determine if rhinoplasty is appropriate and safe. He can also discuss the risks and benefits of surgery, as well as the results that can be expected.

In conclusion, (regarding the question “at what age can a rhinoplasty be done?”) rhinoplasty can be performed on people who have reached facial maturity, generally around 18 years of age. However, there are exceptions, such as cases of congenital deformities or significant respiratory problems, where surgery may be recommended at an earlier age. In any case, the decision to perform rhinoplasty should be made carefully and discussed with an experienced and certified plastic surgeon who operates in a licensed clinic.
