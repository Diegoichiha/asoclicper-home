Successful participation in the International Aesthetic Plastic Surgery Course with our member clinics

ASOCLICPER TeamColombian Association of Plastic, Aesthetic and Reconstructive Surgery Clinics
Leer este artículo en español

Connecting with Assistant Plastic Surgeons

During these four days, our stand was visited by plastic surgeons from all over the world who attended the event. It was an exceptional opportunity to share our mission and highlight the benefits of joining our association. We are pleased to say that we were able to make meaningful connections and spread our commitment to excellence and safety in plastic surgery.

An Essential Role in the Event Agenda

We were not only present at the trade show, but also played an important role in the conferences at the event. Our president, Dr. Álvaro Humberto Arana Núñez, presented his presentation titled «Mammary Tissue Support in Breast Ptosis with and without Implant», which was a resounding success with great participation and a very positive response from the audience. This presentation further cements our commitment to excellence and safety in the profession.

Special Support from Member Clinics

We would like to take this opportunity to express our sincere gratitude to the clinics that are associated with ASOCLICPER and who provided invaluable support during the plastic surgery course. Clinics such as IQ InterQuirófanos, Picasso, Otorrinos and Interplástica played a fundamental role by sending their managers and supporting our activities. Their active participation enriched the discussions and contributed to the success of future affiliations.

The collaboration of these member clinics demonstrates the joint commitment of all parties to promoting excellence and safety in plastic surgery in Colombia. We are excited about the strong partnership we have forged and look forward to working together on future initiatives that will benefit both our members and the broader plastic surgery clinic community.

We appreciate your continued support and trust in our partnership.
