We extend a cordial invitation to you to delve into the enriching editorial of Dr. Gustavo Adolfo Arboleda Palacio, Manager of IQ Quirófanos (IQ Operating Rooms) and active member of ASOCLICPER. Since his incorporation into the Association in 2021, Dr. Arboleda Palacio has brought a valuable contribution.

In the 9th edition, Dr. Arboleda Palacio dedicates a crucial and important segment to our association in his editorial. In it, he highlights the importance of having associated with ASOCLICPER and explores in detail the commitment we have made to promote development and excellence in the sector.

Don’t miss this opportunity to learn about the vision and motivation behind this association from one of our key members. Read now!
