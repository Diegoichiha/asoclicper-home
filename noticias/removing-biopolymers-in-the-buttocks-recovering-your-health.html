Removing biopolymers in the buttocks: recovering your health and aesthetic appearance

ASOCLICPER TeamColombian Association of Plastic, Aesthetic and Reconstructive Surgery Clinics
Leer este artículo en español

Buttock augmentation has gained popularity in recent years, and many people have turned to cosmetic procedures to achieve a curvier, more attractive figure. However, some individuals have experienced complications due to the use of biopolymers for buttock augmentation. These products (biopolymers) are not recommended or approved by health authorities as they can cause adverse effects and health problems. In this article, we will explore the removal of biopolymers in the buttocks, a procedure that seeks to solve the associated risks and restore health and aesthetics.

What are biopolymers and why should they be removed?

Biopolymers are synthetic substances that have been used in the past as fillers to increase buttock volume. However, these products are not biocompatible and can cause adverse reactions in the body, such as inflammation, chronic infections, deformities, migration, and pain. As evidence has accumulated about the risks associated with biopolymers, medical experts and health authorities have recommended their withdrawal to safeguard the health of patients.

The procedure for removing biopolymers in the buttocks:

The removal of biopolymers in the buttocks is a surgical procedure that involves the careful and complete extraction of the previously implanted biopolymers.

Before performing the procedure, an MRI with contrast medium must be performed to accurately verify their location.

Although each case is unique, the procedure is performed under general anesthesia, and may involve skin incisions to access affected tissues.

During the procedure, the plastic surgeon will meticulously remove the biopolymers, making sure to remove as much material as possible. This may require a combination of different techniques, such as liposuction or direct excision of the affected tissues. After removing the biopolymers, the affected area is reconstructed and repaired to achieve a natural and aesthetically pleasing contour.

Importance of seeking qualified professionals:

It is essential to seek a certified and experienced plastic surgeon for the removal of biopolymers in the buttocks. These professionals will have the knowledge and skill necessary to safely and effectively address the complication associated with biopolymers. Additionally, postoperative follow-up and care are essential to ensure adequate recovery and minimize any potential risks or complications.

Benefits of removing biopolymers in the buttocks:

The removal of biopolymers in the buttocks offers a series of significant benefits. In addition to addressing the health risks associated with these materials, the procedure allows:

1. Relieve pain and discomfort caused by biopolymers.

2. Improve the aesthetic appearance and restore the natural shape and contour of the buttocks.

3. Prevent additional migration of biopolymers and possible future complications.

4. Provide a solid foundation for future aesthetic treatments, if desired.

Buttock biopolymer removal is an essential surgical procedure for those who have experienced complications related to the use of these unsafe materials. In addition to addressing health risks, the removal of biopolymers allows the aesthetic appearance to be restored and improves the patients’ quality of life. If you have undergone biopolymer buttock augmentation and are experiencing problems, it is important to seek the guidance of a qualified plastic surgeon to evaluate your case and determine the best treatment plan. Remember that your health and well-being are the most important things and the removal of biopolymers can help you to recover them.
