ASOCLICPER celebrates the approval of Law 2316 as an advance in public safety and health in aesthetic plastic surgery

ASOCLICPER TeamColombian Association of Plastic, Aesthetic and Reconstructive Surgery Clinics
Leer este artículo en español

The Colombian Association of Plastic, Aesthetic and Reconstructive Surgery Clinics (ASOCLICPER), a non-profit entity committed to excellence in medical practice, is pleased with the recent approval of Law 2316. This law, which regulates and sanctions the use of biopolymers represents a fundamental step towards the protection of public health and patient safety in the field of aesthetic plastic surgery.

ASOCLICPER has consolidated itself as a group that unites plastic surgery clinics throughout Colombia, with the aim of raising quality standards, prioritizing patient safety and promoting the sustainable growth of our institutions.

The approval of Law 2316 is a significant achievement in the constant search to guarantee the public health and integrity of those who opt for aesthetic plastic surgery procedures. This regulation will help prevent unnecessary risks and ensure that treatments are carried out with approved and safe substances, avoiding complications that could affect the health of patients.

We firmly ratify our dedication to working in close collaboration with the clinics that are part of our association, thus ensuring rigorous compliance with Law 2316. We recognize our essential position within the medical community and commit to being active agents in the disclosure of accurate information.

We value this legislative progress and reiterate the importance of continuing to advance in the regulation of medical-surgical specialties for aesthetic purposes. The protection and safety of our patients remains our top priority, and we will work tirelessly to maintain and raise the standards of medical practice in the field of aesthetic plastic surgery.
