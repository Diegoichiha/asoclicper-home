Present in the great forum “Colombians Abroad” of FM RCN Radio

ASOCLICPER TeamColombian Association of Plastic, Aesthetic and Reconstructive Surgery Clinics
Leer este artículo en español

On August 3, we participated in the Great Forum of Colombians Abroad, under the theme «Remittances and Foreign Investment, Vallecaucano Growth», organized by LA FM RCN RADIO. Our president, Dr. Álvaro Humberto Arana, led this moment with a presentation titled «Uniting Excellence in Plastic Surgery and Patient Safety.»

The live broadcast allowed Dr. Arana to share information about the work of the Colombian Association of Plastic, Aesthetic and Reconstructive Surgery Clinics (ASOCLICPER) to improve and strengthen institutions in the plastic surgery industry, in addition to guaranteeing high safety standards for those seeking procedures of this type.

«We are looking for new and application of new technologies, and where there is really great safety for our patients,» said Dr. Arana.

Watch the full broadcast of this event, especially the intervention of Dr. Álvaro Humberto Arana, in an effort to spread knowledge, awareness and confidence in high-quality and safe plastic surgery.
