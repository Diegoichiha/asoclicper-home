We celebrate obtaining certification of the DIAN 2022 Special Tax Regime

ASOCLICPER TeamColombian Association of Plastic, Aesthetic and Reconstructive Surgery Clinics
Leer este artículo en español

We are pleased to share news that reinforce our commitment to transparency and compliance with tax regulations. The Colombian Association of Aesthetic and Reconstructive Plastic Surgery Clinics (ASOCLICPER), with NIT (Tax ID Number) 901422858, has successfully obtained the certification of the Special Tax Regime, issued by the National Tax and Customs Directorate (DIAN: Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales ), for the taxable year 2022.

Our efforts to maintain high standards in all aspects of our work have paid off. We thank the Collections Division for its thorough review, which verified that we comply with the requirements established in article 1.2.1.5.1.7. of the Single Regulatory Decree on Tax Matters No. 1625 of 2016. In addition, we have responded exhaustively to all comments submitted by civil society, demonstrating our commitment to transparency and active participation.

This authorization to operate under the Special Tax Regime is a recognition of our integrity and dedication in contributing to the aesthetic and reconstructive plastic surgery sector in Colombia. We remain firm in our mission to provide quality services and promote the sustainable development of our area of specialty.
